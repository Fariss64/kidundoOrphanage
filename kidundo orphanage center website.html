Our Mission & Purpose

Building Futures, One Child at a Time in Zanzibar

Located in the heart of Tunguu, Zanzibar, Kidundo Orphanage Center was established on a simple yet powerful premise: every child, regardless of their background, deserves love, dignity, and a chance to thrive.

Operating in a remote area comes with significant financial and logistical challenges. We rely entirely on compassionate individuals, local support, and international friends to provide basic needs, pay teachers, and maintain a safe housing environment.

"Kidundo gives us a safe place to learn and grow. Because of this sanctuary, I can dream big and aim to become a doctor."
